Overview

I am an undergraduate physics student at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität (LMU) Munich. I am currently in my third semester, taking Quantum Mechanics, Optics & Waves, and Analysis II. In high school, I completed three research projects in observational cosmology, focusing on cosmological parameter inference $(H_0, \Omega_m, \Omega_\Lambda)$ from different standard candles (Cepheids, Type Ia SNe, and GRBs). Through the Youth & Science fellowship, I visited research institutes abroad during summer programs. After first-year university physics, my interests shifted toward theoretical physics, especially classical mechanics.

Selected Research

2025 • Osservatorio Astronomico di Brera (OAB-INAF)

GRB Cosmography & Relativistic Foundations

Constrained cosmological parameters ($\Omega_m$, $\Omega_\Lambda$) via $\chi^2$ minimization of empirical $E_{peak}$–$E_{iso}$ relations in Gamma-Ray Bursts. Analyzed potential synergies with simulated binary neutron star merger populations for future detectors like the Einstein Telescope. Repository

2024–2026 • CAPIBARA Collaboration

The GAMMA Initiative

Serving as project coordinator for team GAMMA. Defined the scientific requirements and payload concepts for a student-led $\gamma$-ray transient observing CubeSat mission.

2024 • Institute of Space Sciences (ICE-CSIC-IEEC)

Type Ia Supernovae as Cosmological Distance Indicators

Derived luminosity distances using ESO (SOFI) infrared imaging alongside ZTF and ATLAS photometry using the SALT2-NIR model. Modeled non-linear cosmic expansion utilizing the Friedmann Equations.

2023 • Fundació Catalunya La Pedrera (Youth & Science)

Cepheid Variable Stars & The Distance Scale

Replicated modern Cepheid distance ladder methods using Konkoly Observatory photometry. Computed a Hubble constant value of approximately $H_0 = 70.26 \pm 7.1 \mathrm{\ km\ s^{-1}\ Mpc^{-1}}$, consistent with contemporary measurements. Repository
