The Broken Toaster

Three siblings try to make breakfast, but the toaster suddenly stops working. They report the following facts:

  • If the toaster is plugged in, the red light turns on.
  • The red light is off.
  • Either the outlet has power, or the toaster fuse is broken.
  • The outlet has no power only if the kitchen breaker is down.
  • The kitchen breaker is up.

What is the most likely direct cause of the toaster not working?

The question

Solution

The most consistent explanation is a broken toaster fuse.

Since the breaker is up, the outlet should still have power. That rules out a power outage at the outlet and leaves an internal toaster failure as the best conclusion.
